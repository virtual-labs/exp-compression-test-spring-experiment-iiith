After completing this experiment, you will be able to:

- Understand the behaviour of a close-coiled helical compression spring under axial loading.
- Measure the compression (deflection) produced for different applied loads.
- Determine the stiffness (spring constant) of the spring.
- Calculate the strain energy stored in the spring during elastic deformation.
- Plot the Load–Deflection graph and interpret its significance.
- Verify Hooke's law for a helical compression spring within the elastic range.
- Relate spring characteristics to practical engineering applications.
